about

anna deforest wrote the novels Our Long Marvelous Dying and A History of Present Illness. they are a neurologist and palliative care physician. their essays on medicine, language, and dying have appeared in Harper's, The Paris Review, The Yale Review, and the New England Journal of Medicine.

A History of Present Illness

little, brown and company · 2022 · a novel

In brilliant, wry, and biting prose, an unsparing meditation on the body, the hope of healing in the face of total loss, and what we do to the dying - and to each other - in the name of care.

Our Long Marvelous Dying

little, brown and company · 2024 · also a novel

A pandemic-hushed city. A young doctor finishing a fellowship in the care of the dying while their own attachments come loose. An ode to life, death, and caregiving - and to the most fundamental human drive there is: the refusal to let go.
